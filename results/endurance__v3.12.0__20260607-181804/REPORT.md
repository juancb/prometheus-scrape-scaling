# 10M/s endurance & first-compaction report

**Prometheus:** 3.12.0  ·  **Box:** AMD EPYC 7R32, 64 vCPU, **124 GiB RAM**, no swap
·  **TSDB:** real NVMe SSD (ext4, `/mnt/tsdb`)  ·  **Date:** 2026-06-07
·  **Data:** `endurance.csv` (1241 ticks @ 10 s)  ·  **Plot:** `endurance-timeseries.png`

## Setup

Run the 10 M samples/s operating point continuously and watch the first **2 h
head-block compaction** (which fires when the head spans 1.5 × 2 h = **3 h**) and
its effect on ingest.

- Prometheus on **16 cores** (`taskset -c 0-15` + `GOMAXPROCS=16`).
- **6.4 M** active series (32 exporters × 200 k), constant set, no churn.
- Fixed `scrape_interval = scrape_timeout = 0.64 s` → 6.4 M / 0.64 = **10.0 M/s**.
- TSDB on **NVMe** (not tmpfs) so compaction does real IO; `retention 24h`.
- Ran **3.5 h** (12 600 s), sampling TSDB/ingest/CPU/RSS/scrape every 10 s.

## Headline: at 10 M/s on 16 cores, the first 2h compaction does not survive contact

| phase | ingest | CPU (16c) | scrape max | RSS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| steady (0–179 min) | **10.10 M/s** | ~85% | ~0.28 s (median) | climbs 12 → 57 GiB |
| **compaction (179–210 min)** | median 9.84, **dips to 5.82 M/s** | **96–100%** | **up to 38.6 s** | **57 → 117 GiB** |

1. **Compaction started exactly on schedule at 3 h00m** (`compact.go: "write block
   started"`, head span = 3.00 h) — for the 2h-aligned block ending 20:00 UTC.
2. **It never finished.** 30.5 minutes later the run ended and the graceful
   shutdown cancelled it mid-flight: `compaction failed: … add series: context
   canceled`. No block was persisted — only a `…tmp-for-creation` dir. On 16
   cores already ~85% busy serving 10 M/s, the background compactor is
   CPU-starved, and the block is enormous (~1.7 h × 6.4 M series ≈ 38 billion
   samples), so it could not keep up.
3. **It nearly OOM'd the box.** RSS jumped from **57 GiB to 117 GiB** within
   minutes of onset and stayed there — **94% of the 124 GiB of RAM**. Compaction
   builds the new block's chunks + 6.4 M-series index in memory *on top of* the
   still-live head. A slightly larger head, higher rate, or longer run would have
   crossed into OOM.
4. **Ingest degraded but didn't collapse.** Between stalls the appender catches
   back up to 10 M/s, so the *median* only falls ~3%, but there are repeated deep
   transient dips to **5.8 M/s** while the CPU is pegged at 96–100%.
5. **Dropped scrapes tripled.** Scrapes exceeding the 0.64 s timeout (= a failed
   scrape / dropped samples for that target) rose from **135/1062 ticks (13%)**
   pre-compaction to **63/179 ticks (35%)** during compaction, with the tail
   exploding from <1 s to **38.6 s** (a multi-second GC/allocation stall under
   memory pressure). `min(up)` sampled at 1 throughout, but that's a sampling
   artifact — the instantaneous 10 s probe misses sub-tick failures; `scrape_max
   > timeout` is the reliable drop signal.

### Secondary finding — periodic maintenance hiccups even before compaction

Pre-compaction steady state is solid at the median (~0.28 s scrapes, 10.1 M/s),
but there's a **regular ~2.5 min cadence of mild scrape-latency spikes**
(0.65–0.95 s, just over the timeout, lasting ~30 s each) — periodic head
maintenance (WAL segment cuts / head-chunk m-mapping). So even without
compaction, the slowest of 32 targets occasionally times out at this rate.

## Why this matters

- The **18 s-window peak rates** from the core-scaling experiment are *fresh-head*
  numbers — they do not pay the compaction tax. **Sustained** 10 M/s on 16 cores
  is not actually steady: every 2 h a compaction arrives that this configuration
  cannot absorb (CPU-starved, near-OOM, degrades ingest).
- Root cause is **resource contention**: ingest and compaction share the same 16
  cores with no headroom, and head + compaction working set ≈ 2× memory. The fix
  is to give compaction room, not to make Prometheus work harder.

## Suggested follow-ups

1. **More cores** — re-run with Prometheus on 24–32 cores so the compactor has
   CPU headroom while 10 M/s ingest continues; see whether the block completes,
   how long it takes, and whether ingest stays clean.
2. **Shard the ingest** — smaller per-instance head ⇒ smaller, faster compaction;
   the production-shaped answer for 10 M/s.
3. **Memory headroom** — size RAM for ~2× the head (head + compaction working
   set), not 1×.
